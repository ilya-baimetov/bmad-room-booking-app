- (direction) Idea from the user (2026-11-13): people want their room bookings in their own calendar app.
- (note) Source: the user's statement of 2026-11-13, kept verbatim with ids S1-S5 in sources/request-2026-11-13.md.
- (note) Code fact: identity is only the person_id cookie set by Pick your name (core/identity.py); anyone can pick any name, so the feed secret is the only thing protecting a feed.
- (note) Code fact: My bookings already lists bookings a person organises or attends (calendar.service.my_bookings -> bookings_service.bookings_for_person).
- (requirement) R1 (S1, S2): From a booking, a person downloads an Add to calendar .ics file; opened in Outlook, Google Calendar or Apple Calendar it adds that booking as one event at its correct UK time.
- (requirement) R2 (S1, S3): Each person has their own private feed address; subscribed in Outlook, Google Calendar or Apple Calendar it shows every booking they organise or attend, at the correct UK time.
- (requirement) R3 (S3): After a booking is changed, the subscribed calendar shows the change on its next refresh; after a booking is cancelled, including by its room closing, it is gone on the next refresh.
- (requirement) R4 (S4): A feed address cannot be guessed or worked out from the person's name, email or id; a request without a valid address gets no bookings.
- (requirement) R5 (S4): A person resets their feed address; afterwards the old address returns no bookings and the new one shows them.
- (requirement) R6 (S3, S4): A person can see and copy their own feed address in the room booker, and only their own.
- (constraint) C1 (S5): No sign-in to Microsoft or Google and no calls to their calendar APIs; the export is files and a feed address only.
- (constraint) C2 (S5): One way only: nothing is read back from any calendar app; the room booker stays the only place to book, change or cancel.
- (constraint) C3 (S1, S2): Standard iCalendar that Outlook, Google Calendar and Apple Calendar all accept as-is; no per-app formats.
- (constraint) C4 (S3): The feed is fetched by calendar apps that send no cookie and do not follow a redirect to Pick your name, so it cannot rely on the current-person cookie.
- (assumption) The feed covers bookings from 30 days ago onward, with no upper limit.
- (assumption) Each event shows the booking title, start and end, the room and office as location, and the organiser.
- (assumption) A cancelled booking simply disappears from the feed; no cancelled events are kept or shown.
- (assumption) How soon changes appear is set by each calendar app's refresh interval (Google Calendar can take hours); the room booker cannot push updates.
- (assumption) The feed address and its reset live on My bookings; Add to calendar sits on a booking's details page.
- (assumption) A downloaded .ics file is a snapshot and does not change when the booking later changes; the feed is the way to stay current.
- (assumption) When a person is made inactive their feed address stops returning bookings.
- (assumption) Room-wide or office-wide calendars (for office managers or room displays) are out of this initiative.
