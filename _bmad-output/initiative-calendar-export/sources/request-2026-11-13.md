# Calendar export request (2026-11-13)

The request as Ilya stated it in the planning conversation, paragraph ids added for citation.

- **S1.** People want their room bookings in the calendar app they already use: Outlook, Google Calendar or Apple Calendar.
- **S2.** Each booking gets an "Add to calendar" download, a standard .ics file.
- **S3.** Each person also gets a private calendar feed address that their calendar app subscribes to. It shows the bookings they made or were invited to, and changes when a booking changes or is cancelled.
- **S4.** The feed address is secret. A person can reset it, and the old address stops working.
- **S5.** No sign-in to Microsoft or Google, and nothing comes back from those calendars: the room booker stays the only place to book.
