- (event) Refinement 2026-11-13: user accepted Sofia's picks on the four open points (tablet setup and removal, freed count placement, freed booking in Your upcoming bookings, freeing go-live); parent R1-R8 and C1-C6 bind as written.
- (requirement) R1 (parent R1): An office manager sets up a tablet once for one room in their office, and can remove it; a room has at most one tablet, so setting up a new one replaces the old. From setup until removal the tablet shows that room's page with no further sign-in.
- (requirement) R2 (parent R2): The room's tablet page shows that room's bookings for today (UK day), and which one is current or next.
- (requirement) R3 (parent R3): Anyone at the tablet checks in a booking with one tap, without saying who they are, from 5 minutes before its start until 15 minutes after; outside that window the tablet offers no check-in for it.
- (requirement) R4 (parent R4): A booking with no check-in by 15 minutes after its start is freed: the rest of its slot, from 15 minutes after its start to its end, can be booked by anyone like any free time.
- (requirement) R5 (parent R5): A freed booking shows as "Freed: nobody checked in" on My bookings and its details page, to its organiser and its attendees alike, and as cancelled in their subscribed calendars; it is distinct from a booking that was cancelled or simply ended. A freed booking cannot be cancelled; Book again still works.
- (requirement) R6 (parent R6): An office manager sees, beside their office's rooms on Manage rooms, how many bookings in their office were freed from Monday to today, UK time.
- (requirement) R7 (parent R7): A booking is freed only if its check-in window, from 5 minutes before its start to 15 minutes after, opens after freeing went live and its room had a tablet set up for the whole of that window; any other booking is never freed.
- (requirement) R8 (parent R8): A booking in a room with a tablet shows, to its organiser and attendees on My bookings and its details page, its check-in window ("Check in at the door, 09:55–10:15") until checked in, freed or the window closes, and "Checked in" once checked in; bookings in rooms with no tablet show what they show today.
- (requirement) R9 (parent R1; AD-8; refinement pick 1): On the tablet's own browser, an office manager signed in by Pick your name chooses "Set up door tablet" on a room's row in Manage rooms and confirms in the row, as for Close; the confirm row names the room and gives the mounting guidance (screen centre about 120 cm from the floor, tablet set never to sleep). Setup clears that browser's person cookie and lands on the door page.
- (requirement) R10 (parent R1; AD-8; refinement pick 1): Each room's row on Manage rooms says whether it has a door tablet and since when; an office manager of that office removes it there, from any device, after a confirm in the row; the removed tablet then shows the not-set-up page.
- (requirement) R11 (parent R6; refinement pick 2): The freed count is one line above the rooms table on Manage rooms, "Bookings freed since Monday: {n}", shown with 0 too.
- (requirement) R12 (parent R5; architecture Deferred; refinement pick 3): A freed booking stays in the Rooms page's "Your upcoming bookings" until it ends, grey and tagged "Freed: nobody checked in" as on My bookings.
- (requirement) R13 (parent R7; refinement pick 4): When freeing went live is the setting FREEING_STARTS_AT (UTC), read through core.settings.get_settings(); while it is unset nothing is freed and no screen says a booking will be freed (no door countdown, no "or the room is freed"), so tablets and check-in can ship first and the setting doubles as an off switch.
