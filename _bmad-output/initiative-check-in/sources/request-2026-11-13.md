# Check-in request, 2026-11-13

The request as Ilya gave it to BMad on 2026-11-13, word for word:

> Start a new initiative called check-in. People check in at a tablet by the room's door, and rooms nobody checks into are freed. I want requirements, UX, architecture, stories and build.

- S1: People check in at a tablet by the room's door.
- S2: Rooms nobody checks into are freed.

Answers given while writing the initiative's requirements are added below, each with its own S-number.

## Answers to Sofia, 2026-11-13, word for word

> This is a new feature for our existing room booking app. Read the deep-recon account of how bookings end today.
>
> Booked rooms often sit empty because nobody turns up.
>
> Each room gets a tablet by its door, showing the room's bookings for today. People check in on the tablet, from 5 minutes before the start until 15 minutes after.
>
> If nobody checks in within 15 minutes of the start, the room is freed: anyone can book the rest of that slot, and the booking shows as freed in its owner's calendar.
>
> Office managers see how many bookings were freed in their office this week.
>
> No QR codes, sensors, badges or notifications in this release. The tablets are ordinary tablets showing a web page, and an office manager sets each one up once.

## Sofia's proposals, accepted 2026-11-13

- S11: (Sofia's proposal, accepted with "All fine.") Anyone at the tablet checks in a booking with one tap, without saying who they are.
- S12: (Sofia's proposal, accepted.) A freed booking shows as "Freed: nobody checked in" on My bookings and the booking's details page, and as cancelled in the organiser's subscribed calendar.
- S13: (Sofia's proposal, accepted.) Rooms whose tablet has not been set up are never freed.
- S14: (Sofia's proposal, accepted.) "This week" for the freed count is Monday to today, UK time, shown to office managers beside their office's rooms on Manage rooms.
- S15: (Sofia's proposal, accepted.) No numeric success target this release: the signal is that unclaimed bookings are freed and managers can see the count.
